How is a Bulin 47 AI video tool different from a face-swap app or a prompt-based AI video generator?

Short answer: Bulin 47 AI is a single-template tool — one front-facing photo in, one Bulin 47 freestyle clip with audio out — whereas face-swap apps paste a face onto any video you supply and prompt-based generators make you describe and iterate a whole scene. It is for people who want this specific trend fast, not a general video studio.

Under the hood

The site states that clips are generated by Seedance 2.0 Mini (bytedance/seedance-2-mini) through the Kie.ai API, and that the template locks the motion while your photo recasts only the person facing the camera.

When it is the wrong tool

If you want a new camera path, a remix, lyrics or a new Bulin 47 song, use something else. It is a fan-made tool, not the official Bulin 47 channel; Bulin 47, Kevin Wai, Ceky Viciny and their labels do not run it.

Q&A

Is it free? Watching the demo clips on the site is free, but a finished MP4 export is paid: one export uses 26 credits, and one-time packs are $9.90 for 2 videos, $19.90 for 5 and $39.90 for 11. There is no subscription, credits do not expire, and a failed job returns its credits.

Does one photo replace both people? No — it recasts the person facing the camera; the person seen from behind stays as in the reference.
